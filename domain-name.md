  My recommendation: newburyparkbasketball.com — it matches the site's actual identity ("Newbury Park Panthers," per the header), reads clearly to parents/boosters, and is easy to say out loud.

  site is a documented step in docs/aws-and-deploy.md/CLAUDE.md's "No custom domain" TODO (ACM cert + CloudFront alias + DNS records) — happy to walk through that with you once you have the domain.
